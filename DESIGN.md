## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from the rich botanical, hand-crafted, and joyous essence of festive gift-making. The core identity radiates warmth, meticulous care, celebratory energy, and heartfelt emotion ("un detallito hecho con amor"). The aesthetic balances floral whimsy with contemporary e-commerce precision.

The visual direction combines **tactile warmth** with **clean modern editorial clarity**:
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit boutique florist, celebratory studio, artisan gifting confectionery.
- **Form Language:** Generously rounded pill surfaces, soft organic contours, petal-like radiuses, and vibrant chromatic accents that frame products without overwhelming readability.
- **Mood:** Vibrant, affectionate, hospitable, optimistic, and handcrafted yet impeccably polished.

## Colors

The palette directly samples the vivid floral composition, luminous letterforms, and lush accents of the reference artwork:

- **Primary (`#D81B60` - Fucsia Pétalo):** Vibrant magenta-rose derived from hibiscus petals and the initial lettering stroke. Used for primary call-to-actions, hero highlights, active states, and focal tags.
- **Secondary (`#7B1FA2` - Violeta Orquídea):** Deep, regal hydrangea purple reflecting the shaded contours of the logo script. Used for secondary interactive actions, key badges, category filters, and premium labels.
- **Tertiary (`#F57C00` - Girasol & Naranja Cálido):** Golden amber-orange drawn from the sunflower center and energetic swooshes. Perfect for special offers, delivery highlights, star ratings, and celebratory triggers.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6F0` - Crema Seda):** A warm, soft linen/creme backdrop that prevents the eye fatigue associated with stark digital white (`#FFFFFF`) while allowing bright floral primaries to sing with warmth.
- **Complementary Accents (Semantic / Utility):**
  - **Follaje Verde (`#2E7D32` / `#4CAF50`):** Success notifications, stock confirmations, freshness indicators.
  - **Celeste Mariposa (`#0288D1` / `#4FC3F7`):** Info alerts, tracked shipments, delivery status icons.
  - **Texto Principal (`#2D1F29` - Ciruela Oscura Profunda):** A high-contrast, deeply saturated plum-charcoal ensuring pristine accessibility and readability over warm cream grounds.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, humanist geometric foundation that echoes the buoyant curves of artisan script and floral petals while preserving clean digital legibility across all screen densities.

- **Headlines & Display:** Heavy weights (700 & 800) create welcoming, joyful title treatments reminiscent of artisan gift packages. Slight negative letter spacing at larger scales keeps headers tight and punchy.
- **Body & Product Descriptions:** Regular weight (400) paired with generous 1.5x+ line heights provides clear readability over cream surfaces.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Semi-bold and bold variants (600 & 700) with slight positive tracking provide crisp badges, category pills, prices, and button CTA text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an open, comfortable 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a fluid 4-column system for mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Outer margin of `2rem` (32px) and column gutter of `1.25rem` (20px). Section blocks use `space-xl` (40px) vertical pacing to emulate breezy, sun-drenched boutique display tables.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and 16px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, prioritizing full-bleed hero banners with curved bottoms and 2-column product card pairings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors the layered physical textures of hand-tied gift ribbons, stacked wrapping papers, and layered floral bouquets:

- **Ambient Tinted Shadows:** Shadows avoid harsh grayscale tones, utilizing warm plum and terracotta undertones (`rgba(66, 18, 48, 0.06)` up to `rgba(66, 18, 48, 0.14)`).
- **Surface Layering:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Soft Warm Cream (`#FAF6F0`).
  - **Cards & Enclosures:** Crisp Warm White (`#FFFDFB`) with subtle borders in translucent plum (`rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.12)`) and light ambient diffusion (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(90, 20, 60, 0.06)`).
  - **Floating Navigation & Modals:** Soft frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with elevated drop shadows (`0 12px 36px -4px rgba(66, 18, 48, 0.12)`).
- **Interactive Hover:** Elements gently elevate with an expanded bloom shadow tinted toward primary fucsia (`0 10px 24px -2px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is defined by `pill-shaped` and petal-soft geometry (`level 3`), celebrating the organic outlines of flowers, butterfly wings, and flowing hand-lettered calligraphy.

- **Buttons & Tags:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` border radius) providing an inviting, touchable feel.
- **Cards & Image Containers:** Rounded with `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) corners, often framing photography like artisan gift boxes.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** Soft rounded-lg (1rem / 16px) or pill contours with generous inner breathing room.
- **Accent Badges & Icons:** Encased in circular or squircle containers that evoke stamped sealing wax or botanical badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid vibrant magenta (`#D81B60`), white bold text, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers a warm glow shadow (`0 8px 20px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined in violet hortensia (`#7B1FA2`) or filled with light translucent lilac (`rgba(123, 31, 162, 0.08)`), text colored `#7B1FA2`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Flat warm cream base with primary colored text, gentle underline on hover.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills (`space-xs` vertical, `space-md` horizontal). Inactive chips feature a pale cream card background with subtle border (`#EBD9D0`). Active chips display solid primary fucsia or violet with white text and a miniature floral or butterfly checkmark icon.

### Cards (Product & Gift Showcase)
- Background: Warm white (`#FFFDFB`).
- Radius: `rounded-xl` (24px).
- Border: 1px subtle warm border (`rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.1)`).
- Image Frame: Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 with soft overflow-hidden inner radius (`20px`). Includes top-right floating wishlist button (white circle with animated magenta heart).
- Price & Metadata: High-contrast title in plum (`#2D1F29`), price highlighted in bold primary fucsia, and seasonal badges (e.g., "Para Cumpleaños", "Artesanal") in sunflower amber (`#F57C00`).

### Form Inputs
- Rounded pill or `rounded-lg` (16px), background `#FFFDFB`, border 1.5px `#E8DCD5`. Focus state activates a vibrant magenta border (`#D81B60`) with a soft 3px halo (`rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes feature rounded corners (6px), filled with primary fucsia on select.
- Radio buttons feature concentric rings with a central fucsia dot on warm white.

### Specialty Artisan Components
- **Gift Note Card (Dedicatoria Personalizada):** A deckled-edge paper styled surface with soft italic script font sample, surrounded by a delicate botanical border.
- **Occasion Picker:** Circular icon tiles showcasing stylized floral, butterfly, and celebratory gift motifs with soft pastel backdrop rings.
